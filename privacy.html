Privacy Policy Smart Email Builder

Qazi Games · Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app and web version of Smart Email Builder, published by Qazi Games ("we", "us"). The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not run servers that collect your data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or sell your personal information. The app keeps its data on your own device only, using your browser's local storage: your preferences (the name you type in the "From" box, language, tone, theme) and up to 8 recent email drafts. This data is not sent to us. It stays on your device until you delete it.

2. Services used only when you press a button

  • Translate (🌐 ➜): the text of your email and its subject are sent to the free MyMemory translation service (mymemory.translated.net) to be translated. This happens only when you press the button.
  • Polish English (✨): the text of your email is sent to the LanguageTool grammar-check service (languagetool.org). This happens only when you press the button.
  • Voice (🎙️): the microphone is used only after you tap the microphone button and allow access. Speech recognition is provided by your device's browser or speech service (for example, Google), which may process the audio on its servers. We do not receive or store your audio.
  • Read aloud (🔊): uses your device's built-in text-to-speech.
  • Open in Mail (📧): opens your own email app with your text. We never send emails for you.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. Do not enter confidential information if you do not want it processed by them. If you never press Translate or Polish, your text is never sent anywhere.

3. Hosting

The app loads from GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, under GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not receive this data.

4. Ads, purchases and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user accounts and no analytics or tracking tools.

5. Children

Smart Email Builder is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete drafts with the 🗑️ button in the drafts list (🕘).
  • To remove all saved data, clear the app's storage in Android: Settings → Apps → Smart Email Builder → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

7. Security

Smart Email Builder is loaded over HTTPS. Because your saved data stays on your device, its protection also depends on your device's security.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
